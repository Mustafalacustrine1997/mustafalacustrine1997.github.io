## 🪄 How CosMMD Works (The Simple Version)

CosMMD combines several amazing AI technologies behind the scenes to make this happen:

- **Nano Banana Pro** takes your photo and analyzes your appearance – facial features, body shape, clothing style.
- **Tripo 3D** converts that analysis into a complete 3D model of you.
- **Blender** (included in the package) handles the final rigging – adding the digital skeleton and joints.
- **MMD dance data** gets applied to your 3D model, and just like that, you're dancing.

The entire process preserves your unique appearance. If you wear glasses, the 3D you wears glasses. If you have a specific hairstyle, the 3D you has that exact haircut. Even your fingers are individually articulated for realistic hand movements.
